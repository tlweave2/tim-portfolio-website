VidlyAI

AI video editor that turns raw phone clips into captioned vertical reels. A Python worker runs a nine-stage pipeline: faster-whisper word-level transcription, OpenCV face tracking, and speaker detection, with Claude returning an edit decision list that is validated against the source clips and rendered with FFmpeg. Web, iOS, and Android apps sit on a Spring Boot and PostgreSQL API.
